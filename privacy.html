Privacy Policy

Talkmy, the AI for everyone. In effect from November 16, 2026.

The short version: Talkmy collects nothing. There is no account and no sign-in. What you say and what you show it are not sent to us. There is no server waiting to receive them.

Listening

Talkmy only hears you while you are holding the Talk button. The microphone is never on at any other time, and Talkmy never listens in the background.

Your capsule

Talkmy remembers a few things so it can be helpful: your language, the names of your people, and how you like answers given. Those live in a small file, called a capsule, stored on your own phone. It never leaves your phone. If you delete Talkmy, the capsule is deleted with it.

Your voice

Turning your speech into words, and reading answers aloud, is done by Apple's speech technology built into your iPhone. For some languages, Apple does that work on Apple's own computers, under Apple's privacy policy. Talkmy itself only receives the words, on your phone.

Your photos

A photo you take or choose is used only for the question you ask with it, on your phone. Photos are never uploaded anywhere.

No tracking

Talkmy has no advertising, no analytics, and no trackers. Nothing about how you use it is reported to anyone.

Everyone

Because Talkmy collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new words will appear on this page with a new date, before they take effect.
