Privacy Policy

Talkmy, the AI for everyone. In effect from November 16, 2026.

The short version: Talkmy has no account and no sign-in, no advertising, and no tracking. What Talkmy remembers about you stays in a small file on your own phone. When you ask a question, your words, and a photo if you are asking about one, are sent to Google's AI service to make the answer. We keep no copy of them, and nothing is tied to your name.

Listening

Talkmy only hears you while you are holding the Talk button. The microphone is never on at any other time, and Talkmy never listens in the background.

How answers are made

When you ask a question, Talkmy sends your question to Google's Gemini AI service, through Google's Firebase, and the answer comes back to your phone. If you are asking about a photo, the photo is sent with your question. Your question is used to make your answer. Under the terms of the paid service Talkmy uses, your questions are not used to train Google's models. Talkmy itself is run by its maker, who keeps no server copy of your questions, your photos, or the answers. To reach Google's service, the app uses a random number for the app install. It is not your name, and there is no account for it to belong to.

Your voice

Turning your speech into words, and reading answers aloud, is done by Apple's speech technology built into your iPhone. For some languages, Apple does that work on Apple's own computers, under Apple's privacy policy. What is sent onward to be answered is the words, not a recording of your voice.

Answering on the phone itself

Some newer iPhones can answer questions on the phone, without sending anything out. Where Talkmy offers that choice, it will explain it in plain words and remember what you chose. In that mode, your questions are answered on your phone and nothing is sent to Google. A photo cannot be answered in that mode.

Your capsule

Talkmy remembers a few things so it can be helpful: your language, the names of your people, and how you like things, such as bigger words on the screen or a slower voice. Those live in a small file, called a capsule, stored on your own phone. It is never sent to us. If you delete Talkmy, the capsule is deleted with it.

Your photos

A photo you take or choose is held in your conversation for the question you ask with it, and sent with that question to be answered, as described above. Talkmy does not save photos into your photo library, and keeps no copy of its own.

A family member for assistance

Talkmy may ask whether you would like to add a family member who can help you when needed. If you add one, their name, phone number, and email are kept in your capsule, on your phone, and nowhere else. Talkmy does not contact them on its own.

No tracking

Talkmy has no advertising, no analytics, and no trackers. Nothing about how you use it is reported to anyone.

Everyone

Talkmy asks no one for an account, a name, or an age. The same rules on this page apply to everyone who uses it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new words will appear on this page with a new date, before they take effect.
